Apple updates App Review Guidelines for random/anonymous chat apps
Apple clarified that random/anonymous chat apps fall under Guideline 1.2 (UGC). Here’s what that means operationally.
Original announcement (source):
Apple Developer News -
“Updated App Review Guidelines now available”
(Feb 6, 2026)
https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=d75yllv4
What changed
Apple updated the App Review Guidelines to clarify that apps with
random or anonymous chat
are subject to
Guideline 1.2 (User-Generated Content)
.
Why this matters
If your app has anonymous / random chat, expect scrutiny around:
moderation and safety features
reporting + blocking flows
abuse handling
policies and enforcement
Even if “chat” isn’t the core product, adding a chat feature can move you into a stricter review lane.

https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=d75yllv4
