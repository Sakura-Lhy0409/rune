Meet Geeps 2.0: now create and edit images with OpenRouter, use built-in web tools, share content into chats, customize themes and fonts, and enjoy faster conversations!
Why Geeps
- Lightweight, native AI chat for iPhone, iPad and Mac
- One app for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, and compatible APIs
- Bring your own API keys (BYOK)
- Pay only for provider usage instead of multiple AI subscriptions
- Keep chats synced across devices with iCloud
What you can do
- Chat with the latest models as they appear in provider APIs
- Use built-in web search and read public web pages
- Generate and edit images with supported OpenAI and OpenRouter models
- Generate images inline in supported conversations
- Share URLs, text, images, and supported files directly into new chats via share sheet
- Attach PDFs, documents, and photos to chat context
- Save and reuse prompts with Prompt Library
- Add custom OpenAI-compatible providers and connect to local models
- Tune reasoning, temperature, token limits, and other supported parameters
- Customize separate light and dark themes, chat and code fonts, provider icons, and chat width
- Export conversations to Markdown
Geeps Plus
Some tools and features require a one-time Geeps Plus upgrade. Geeps Plus is not a subscription.
Important
An API key is required to use Geeps. If you are new to API keys, a quick setup guide is available at geeps.app.
Privacy
Geeps does not route chats through developer-operated servers. Chats stay on your devices and can sync through your private iCloud account. Requests go directly to the providers and tool services you configure.

Custom Endpoints + Image Generation
10/12/2025
Dylan G
Geeps is, quite simply, the first iOS app that actually gets it when it comes to working with custom AI endpoints. After years of cobbling together browser workarounds, obscure proxy setups, and unstable shortcuts, Geeps finally delivers a seamless, native experience for those of us who need fine-grained control over our AI infrastructure.Unlike other apps that force you into closed ecosystems or barely support basic API customization, Geeps handles custom endpoints with remarkable reliability. Whether you’re routing through OpenAI, Cloudflare AI Gateway, LiteLLM, or your own local instance, it just works. No crashes, no throttling surprises, no mysterious “invalid request” errors. The configuration interface is elegant and developer-friendly, but still accessible to non-technical users who simply want to plug in their key and get started.What truly sets Geeps apart, however, is that it doesn’t stop at text. It’s one of the very few iOS clients that integrates image generation alongside LLM endpoints, all without requiring external webviews or janky third-party tools. You can generate images directly within the chat interface, making it a one-stop shop for ideation, content creation, and technical experimentation.For researchers, artists, and AI tinkerers, Geeps is the missing piece: a stable, thoughtfully designed client that respects your endpoints, expands your creative possibilities, and feels at home on iOS. It’s early days, but the foundation is rock-solid…and frankly, no other app currently comes close at doing what it is supposed to do.
