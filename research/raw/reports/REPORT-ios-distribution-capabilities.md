# iOS Distribution Alternatives & Unlocked Capabilities (researched ~Oct 2026)

## 1. Free Apple ID (Personal Team) — **CONFIRMED** ([Apple: Developer account overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/basics/about-your-developer-account))
- **Up to 10 App IDs**, which **expire after 7 days** — note: Apple's wording is "10 App IDs *which expire after 7 days*", not "10 per 7 days".
- **Up to 3 devices**, expiring after 7 days; **up to 3 apps per device**.
- Profiling: "Provisioning profiles that enable apps to be installed on a device will expire **7 days from issuance**. You'll need to rebuild and reinstall."
- Free tier **lacks** Certificates/Identifiers/Profiles, App Store Connect, TestFlight, Xcode Cloud → **no push, no iCloud, no App Groups, no restricted entitlements**. On-device Xcode testing *is* included.
- **Paid $99/yr**: 1-year profiles; Ad Hoc to **100 devices per product family per membership year**; list reset at renewal; devices purged 180 days after expiry ([Devices overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/devices/devices-overview/)).

## 2. Ad Hoc — **CONFIRMED, still fully supported in 2026**
- **100 devices per product family per membership year** (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Vision Pro, iPod touch). Disabling a device does **not** free a slot.
- Requires registered UDIDs + a distribution certificate + 1-year profile ([Create an ad hoc provisioning profile](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/provisioning-profiles/create-an-ad-hoc-provisioning-profile/)).

## 3. TestFlight — **CONFIRMED** ([TestFlight](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/), [ASC Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview/))
- **10,000 external testers**; **100 internal testers** (ASC users); **up to 100 builds**; **90 days** per build; **first build requires App Review** (subsequent builds may not); testers get **up to 30 devices**; **no UDIDs/profiles needed**.

## 4. Enterprise Program — **CONFIRMED** ([Enterprise Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enterprise/))
- Fee is **USD 299/membership year** (not $99). Criteria: **100+ employees**, **legal entity** (no DBAs/branches), **D-U-N-S number**, **publicly available website** on an org domain, systems ensuring only employees download, and passing a **verification interview + continuous evaluation**.
- Explicitly "**only for the internal use and distribution of proprietary apps**". Apple's own dropdown tells public-app distributors to enroll in the Apple Developer Program instead.
- **Verdict: distributing a general-purpose AI agent to outside users via Enterprise is a direct violation of the program terms.** Precedent: Apple **revoked Facebook's and Google's enterprise certificates in Jan/Feb 2019** for exactly this ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/01/facebook-google-scandal/), [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2019/01/apple-shuts-down-googles-internal-ios-apps-just-like-facebook/)). **Not viable.**

## 5. EU DMA — **CONFIRMED** ([Changes for apps in the EU](https://developer.apple.com/support/apps-in-the-eu/), [Apple Newsroom, Aug 2026](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-announces-changes-for-apps-in-the-european-union/))
- ADPLA updated **18 Aug 2026**; unified EU terms effective **1 Oct 2026**. **Core Technology Fee → Core Technology Commission (5%)** on digital transactions outside the App Store; Initial Acquisition Fee and Store Services Fee **eliminated**.
- Rates: App Store **26% / 15%**; alternative payments **20% / 10%**; store services **15% / 10%**; **CTC 5%**.
- **Eligibility (any one, from 1 Oct 2026)** for *both* operating a marketplace and **Web Distribution**: moderate D&B financial-stability score; publicly traded/owned; VC funding from an established firm; licensed-accountant audit; government/education/nonprofit with fee waiver; **USD 1,000,000 standby letter of credit**; or **1,000,000 first annual installs worldwide**. A legal entity/EU establishment is **no longer required**.
- Shipping flow: Alternative EU Terms Addendum → register **Developer ID** with the marketplace → enter **marketplace token** (ASC → Users and Access → Integrations → Marketplace) → select apps for alternative distribution → **Notarization** (Review Type = Notarization; baseline review vs. *Notarization Review Guidelines*, a subset of the ARG) → host the **Alternative Distribution Package** → publish a source JSON ([AltStore dev guide](https://faq.altstore.io/developers/distribute-with-altstore-pal)). Fall 2026 adds a **90-day travel allowance** outside the EU.

## 6. AltStore PAL — **CONFIRMED live**: "**available only in the EU, Japan, and Brazil**" ([altstore.io](https://altstore.io/), [FAQ](https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-pal/what-is-altstore-pal)). The **€1.50/yr** subscription (levied to cover Apple's CTF) was **removed** with an Epic Games MegaGrant ([teknoblog](https://www.teknoblog.com/altstore-pal-epic-games-destegiyle-abonelik-ucretini-kaldiriyor/)) — secondary source. Competitive picture: **Setapp Mobile is closing (~17 months in)**, citing EU terms that are "complex and still evolving" ([ithome](https://www.ithome.com/0/913/688.htm)); **Epic Games Store iOS** was still awaiting Apple's "final approval" ([teknoblog](https://www.teknoblog.com/altstore-pal-epic-games-destegiyle-abonelik-ucretini-kaldiriyor/)); Aptoide/Skich status **[不确定]** — not verified.

## 7. CRITICAL — what each channel actually unlocks
- **(a) JIT: no distribution channel grants it to a general-purpose app.** `com.apple.security.cs.allow-jit` is **macOS-only (10.7+)**, per Apple's own platform metadata ([docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/BundleResources/Entitlements/com.apple.security.cs.allow-jit)) — it does **not** exist for iOS. iOS JIT is reachable *only* via the **Web Browser Engine / Embedded Browser Engine Entitlements**, which require the app be "**distributed solely on iOS and/or iPadOS in the European Union**", score **90% Web Platform Tests + 80% Test262**, and accept heavy security/privacy obligations; Apple then supplies "just-in-time compilation, multiprocess support, and more" ([Alternative browser engines in the EU](https://developer.apple.com/support/alternative-browser-engines/)). **This is a browser-only door — a coding agent cannot qualify.**
- **The only real-world JIT route** is debugger-attach: **StikDebug/StikJIT** + a `jitterbugpair` `.mobiledevicepairing` file + StosVPN, requiring **one-time Mac/PC setup** ([AltStore: Enabling JIT](https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-classic/enabling-jit)). AltStore's own comparison table: **AltStore Classic = "Supports JIT ✅", AltStore PAL = "❌"** — i.e. *marketplace* distribution loses JIT; classic sideloading keeps it, at the cost of 7-day expiry and a 3-app limit on free IDs.
- **(b) Files outside the container: prohibited.** Guideline **2.5.2**: apps "**may not read or write data outside the designated container area**" ([ARG](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)).
- **(c) Background execution**: `BackgroundTasks`/BGTaskScheduler only — "tasks requiring **minutes** to complete"; no persistent daemon ([Background Tasks](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/BackgroundTasks)).
- **(d) fork/exec: unavailable.** 2.5.2 also bars apps from "**download, install, or execute code which introduces or changes features or functionality**"; the only carve-out is educational apps letting users run **viewable/editable source**. **Guideline 4.7** does permit "**HTML5 and JavaScript mini apps**… chatbots, and plug-ins", but 4.7.2 forbids exposing native platform APIs without Apple's prior permission.

## iOS 26/27 + on-device AI — **CONFIRMED**
- **Core AI (iOS 27.0+)**: "Run AI models in your app on Apple silicon" — CPU/GPU/**Neural Engine**, `.aimodel` format via Core AI Optimization + PyTorch Extensions, `coreai-build`, [apple/coreai-models](https://github.com/apple/coreai-models) ([Core AI](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/CoreAI)). **This is the sanctioned local-LLM runtime — no JIT required.**
- **Foundation Models** now gives "access to **any** large language model": rebuilt on-device System Language Model, **Private Cloud Compute**, **Core AI**, and **MLX**; upcoming **Anthropic and Google** integrations; a `LanguageModelExecutor` lets providers ship models as Swift packages; `@Generable` + `Tool` enable tool-calling agents ([Foundation Models](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/FoundationModels), [WWDC26 #339](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/339/)). Requires an Apple Intelligence-capable device.
- **No Apple "AI apps" special-entitlement program was found** — no evidence of one exists.
- **Japan**: Apple announced MSCA-driven changes **17 Dec 2025** (shipped with **iOS 26.2**): alternative marketplaces + notarization, alternative browser engines, and the same **5% CTC** ([Apple Newsroom Japan](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/12/apple-announces-changes-to-ios-in-japan/)). ARG 2.5.6 now points to browser-engine entitlements "for the **EU and Japan**". Brazil: AltStore PAL lists it, but no Apple public regime page was found → **[不确定]**.

**Bottom line:** every official channel caps out at "container-sandboxed, no JIT, no fork/exec, minutes-scale background, no downloaded code" — so a full-power agent must run on-device inference via **Core AI/Foundation Models** with an *interpreted/embedded* tool runtime under 4.7, or leave the platform.
